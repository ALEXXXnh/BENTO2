KITTY ROLLS Igredients: 2 cups of sushi rice 1 bit of salt 1 Nori sheet 1 soja 1 cheese sheet 1 ham sheet 1 bit of sesamo seeds 1 thin tortilla Instructions: Cook the rice until it is done and mix with a little salt. Separate into 4 portions. Mix soy sauce in one, and sesame seeds in another. Form into barrel-shaped balls. Cut small ham ears and use nori seaweed for the mustache, eyes, and nose. Cover the other side with a thin omelet, with nori seaweed, with a slice of cheese, with ham or another garnish.

KARAAGE Igredients: 400 gr Chicken (2 chicken breasts) 1 clove Garlic 2 cm Ginger 2 tablespoons Soy Sauce 4 tablespoons Cornstarch quantity as needed Oil for frying (approx. 250 - 500ml) to taste Lemon Instructions: Prepare the Ingredients: Cut the chicken into 4 cm pieces. Finely chop the garlic. Peel and grate the ginger. Tenderize the Chicken: Pierce the chicken with a fork. (This helps the flavor penetrate the chicken and keeps it tender.) Marinate the Chicken: In a bowl, thoroughly mix the chicken, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce. Marinate for at least 15 minutes. (It's recommended to refrigerate it in the summer to prevent spoilage.) Prepare for Frying: Drain the chicken in a mesh colander to remove excess moisture. Add the cornstarch and mix well by hand. (Tip: You can use a ziploc bag to avoid getting your hands dirty.) Fry the Chicken: In a pot, add oil (approximately 3 cm deep) and heat it. Test the oil's temperature by placing a wooden spoon in it. If small bubbles appear, the oil is hot enough. Add the chicken to the oil and fry for 4-5 minutes. Finish and Serve: Once the chicken is golden brown, remove it from the oil and place it on paper towels to absorb excess oil. Serve with lemon to taste, and enjoy!
